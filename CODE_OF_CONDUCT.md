# Code of conduct

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to make the thing better, because they
almost always are.

Concretely, in a project this small:

- Critique the code, not the person who wrote it. "This breaks in dark mode" is
  useful; "did you even look at this" is not, and it is also usually wrong.
- Say what you checked. A report that names the browser, the screen reader or the
  reproduction is worth ten that do not.
- If you are told your change is not going in, that is not a verdict on you. Small
  projects say no to good ideas all the time, for reasons of scope.

Harassment, personal attacks, and any of the usual varieties of contempt are not
welcome here and will get you removed from the repository.

Report anything to the maintainer via a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/lukesgood/deep-ui/security/advisories/new)
if it is sensitive, or a plain issue if it is not.
